## Checked and fine
- Starts do not repeat. The desire persists once set; a delivered start settles (Charging, Starting or Complete count as satisfied); a refusal excludes the provider across desires (newDesire keeps non-UNSUPPORTED exclusions). "Complete" blocks the loop until uncompleted() turns it into "Stopped" after our limit write.
- No start/stop/start loop at a single point: a stop desire blocks admission and the loop (but see the eviction and stale-stop findings).
- The Gen2 withdraw skip stops the clear-and-reset cycle. If room flips, withdraw clears the desire and the loop sets it again. Within 60 s of a send it keeps the record; after that a resend happens only if the car still isn't drawing, which a delivered start ends.
- The parity row whose meaning flipped (unnamed 00 starts the car plugged in by elimination) is justified: there is one candidate, it is named by VIN, there is no fan-out, and paid is still (0,0,0).
- The setup() 32 A change is justified: base's tests relied on "no offered entry" meaning "no refusal".
- The stop tests that pin 0 A only avoid an unrelated setAmps.
- Comments say why and carry no history; Python 3.8 is fine.
