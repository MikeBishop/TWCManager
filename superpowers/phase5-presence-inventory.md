## 0. The shape in one paragraph

The presence branch's `VehiclePresence` (1,458 lines) plus `PresenceSources` (159) and `PresenceHandlers` (197) did three jobs. The vehicle model already does two of them:
- **Recording evidence**: the registry does this now. `vehicle.reported(field)` holds each source's latest observation with its time, and `age()` honours a live feed.
- **Delivering a restore**: the reconciler does this now. BLE first. A paid provider only after the free channel has failed for `PAID_AFTER[CHARGE_LIMIT] = 300` s, which is the branch's `BLE_GONE_RESTORE_AFTER`. Hourly retry through `RESTORE_RETRY`. Read before every write.

What's left is the third job: **a pure derivation `presence(vehicle) -> HOME | PRESUMED | NOT_HOME` over registry fields**, plus `pickPluggedCar`. There's no thread, no observation deque, no nominations, no probes, and no `DepartingRecord`. The only state is a small per-VIN memo in the control tick, which turns a change in derived presence into a plug-in or unplug decision. Everything below follows from that shape. Its home is a new `lib/TWCManager/Vehicle/Presence.py`. It takes over the presence half of `Reconcile/Targets.py` and the blind rules in `EVSEInstance/VehicleEndpoint.py`.

Derivation rules (all from the plug spec, unchanged):
- **Strong evidence decides on its own.** That means `twcVin` set, or a `pluggedIn` reading from `Gen2TWC`, `Gen3TWC` or `TeslaBLE`.
- **Weak evidence counts only while the car is here.** Weak means `pluggedIn` from `TeslaMateVehicle`, or plugged derived from a `chargingState` by TeslaMate, Fleet Telemetry or TeslaAPI (`Disconnected` means unplugged; `""`, `Unknown` and `SNA` say nothing; HA counts only `Charging`). "Here" means inside the radius by the newest `lat`/`lon`, `bleReach` True within 300 s, or a TWC naming the car.
- **Strong outranks weak** for 300 s.
- **A weak plugged reading ages out** after `age("pluggedIn") > 3600`. A live feed keeps it current through `age()`.
- **No plug evidence at all since start means PRESUMED.** PRESUMED counts as home for limits and charging, but gets no Fleet amps endpoint.
- **NOT_HOME is never presumed again** until plug evidence returns.

## 4. Questions only you can answer (most important first)

1. **Drop the `vehicles[0]` fallback for a VIN-less Gen2 (E4)?** Today an anonymous session with no plugged candidate rates the first TeslaAPI-listed car. That can be a paid Fleet rate to a car that isn't on the TWC. This reverses a ruling: Phase 3 (progress.md:173, slim Task 12b I2) kept main's `vehicles[0]` when no plugged car was known, because nothing better existed then. What's changed is that presence now supplies candidates and attribution, and PRESUMED cars get no Fleet amps path. **Recommend drop.** With presence, an unattributed VIN-less plug rates only candidates whose latest reading shows them plugged while here. If there are none, the TWC carries the limit alone, as it did before the vehicle model. Cost if wrong: a single-car Fleet-only household on a VIN-less TWC in mode 2 loses car-side rate control until the car's telemetry or a read says plugged. Mode 1 is unaffected.
2. **When does a Gen2 plug session end?** This is a Phase 4 interface change. On phase4, `endPlugSession`, which clears `twcVin` (the strong "a TWC names this car" evidence), runs 5 s after charging stops (`TWCMaster.py:1340`), not at unplug. If that stays alongside T1, a VIN-reading TWC loses its strong evidence at charge-complete while the car is still plugged in. Presence then rests on weak sources or PRESUMED for the rest of the visit. **Recommend**: `twcVin` and `endPlugSession` move to T1's debounced unplug, while `plugSessions` (the lift counter) and `recordVehicleSessionEnd` (kWh) stay at charge stop. This touches `test_registry_plug_sessions.py` and the lift tests. The alternative, keeping both ends, means "a TWC naming the car" expires at every charge-complete.
3. **Drop the `bleSilent` UMC exception (R12)?** On the branch, a car plugged in at home that BLE couldn't reach got a Fleet amps endpoint even beside a plugged VIN-less TWC. It needs active BLE probing (two failed probes, re-probes) and it's the only presence rule that opens a paid amps path. **Recommend drop.** Such a car is blinded while the VIN-less TWC is plugged in, as on phase4 today. It's rated normally whenever the TWC is named, idle-unnamed with no telemetry plug, or absent. Saves about 40 lib lines and 11 tests.
4. **No active presence probing (R13)?** **Recommend no probes.** Evidence comes only from reads that already happen (reconciler read-before-write, `updateChargeAtHome`, TeslaMate, TWC heartbeats, user-requested BLE scans, and every BLE contact, which now observes `bleReach`). The trade-off: a BLE-only car with no TWC VIN and no telemetry that is unplugged and driven off is noticed only when a reconciler read or a scan reaches it. If it's held, the restore still goes out: the next policy pass runs `_restoreDeparted`, and the attempt's BLE failure then leads to paid after 300 s where allowed. The alternative is porting `probePresence` (body-controller-state, sleep-safe) on a slow timer for held cars only, about +40.
5. **Home location: seed and move only from a TWC-named car (R20)?** phase4 seeds home from the first fix any car reports (TeslaAPI with a stale-fix guard; HA without one) and re-seeds through a paid `update_location` when a TWC names a car that seems away. **Recommend**: seed or move only when a TWC names the car and the registry already holds a fix under 600 s old. Never read for it, and never seed from the first fix. Fleet-only installs with no TWC VIN set home from the Settings page, which already exists (`HTTPControl.py:1060-1075`). This removes one paid read path. Cost if wrong: a Fleet-only user who never set home has no geofence until they set it, so weak plug evidence never qualifies and the car stays PRESUMED (limits and charging still apply, no Fleet amps endpoint).
6. **Fleet-only departure timing (R31).** Without a TWC VIN, BLE or telemetry, an unplug is seen only at the existing 5/20/45-minute `checkDeparture` reads after a power drop. Those are the same paid reads as phase4, now limited to held cars with no strong source. **Recommend accept.** The restore lands minutes after departure, not seconds, and costs no more than today.
7. **One `statusDeferral` value (R19).** The ladder (300 s home, 1,800 s parked away, 3,600 s far) exists to poll location while away, which the plug model no longer needs. **Recommend** a single 300 s deferral for cars at home, with away cars not polled at all (they aren't on `present()`'s list for `updateChargeAtHome`). Saves about 12 lines; no new paid reads, and fewer.
8. **Accept the reconciler's restore schedule in place of the branch's 600/1,200/2,400/3,600 backoff (R28)?** **Recommend yes**: one schedule in lib. The scenario `test_fleet_only_failed_remote_restore_is_retried_after_600_s` becomes "retried per `RESTORE_RETRY` with the paid cap".
9. **Keep `/api/checkArrival` and `/api/checkDeparture`?** They're undocumented in `docs/`. **Recommend keep** both as 202: arrival as a no-op re-evaluation, departure running the R31 read. That's 0 lines. Dropping them saves 12 but could break a user script.

## Presence model — final (user, 2026-10-18; supersedes the ARRIVING/four-gate/D1/D3 bullets above where they conflict)
- Three values computed fresh from evidence at each pass (no stored state machine): HOME (strong: plugged in here — TWC through the gates or a pushed plug report with a fix inside the radius — or BLE contact), NOT_HOME (fix outside the radius, or confirmed departure), PRESUMED (anything else: startup, fix inside the radius, held car). ARRIVING is PRESUMED.
- Sticky: HOME stays HOME while the car is plugged in or asleep (pushed or our own read); only movement evidence re-opens it (pushed state=driving / shift out of P, pushed unplug, TWC unplug through the gates, BLE contact lost). Same for "inside radius and driving": a pushed parked/plugged/asleep confirms without another read.
- No polling: nothing reads a car just to learn presence. Late changes come only from pushed sources (TeslaMate, Fleet Telemetry) and from our own attempts. Fleet+UMC with no push: a late plug-in waits until there is a message. (Drops the 20-min read rule.)
- Applicability rule for every message, before any paid command: apply/start/amps need the car home; a restore needs it gone.
- Send sequence for a PRESUMED car with a message: (1) BLE sleep-safe ping (`body-controller-state`, VCSEC, does not wake) — answers ⇒ HOME ⇒ command over BLE (waking only for messages allowed to wake: raises, starts); (2) no answer ⇒ paid path: free online check (vehicles/{vin}); wake only if the message may wake (never lowerings or restores); (3) the existing read-before-write also fetches location and drive state (same vehicle_data request): outside radius ⇒ NOT_HOME, nothing sent; inside and parked, or plugged here ⇒ send (HOME); inside and driving ⇒ hold, stays PRESUMED; no usable location ⇒ use the read's plug state (plugged ⇒ send; not ⇒ hold, log once). Paid fallback clock starts at HOME. This reverses D1 (a presumed car with a real message may be woken and read on the paid path, close to main); D3: PRESUMED commands per this sequence.
- Unplug gates reduce to two cheap filters: (a) the slave's reportedAmpsMax drops >0 → 0 at the 00 onset (logged at every onset); (b) live telemetry plugged_in=true vetoes. The anti-loop property comes from the restore being an ordinary message: BLE contact proves home ⇒ restore cancelled; restores never wake on the paid path.
- Near-home false positives: record the car's own AC maximum (charge_current_request_max) from reads that happen anyway; charging power above it at the car's voltage ⇒ DC ⇒ not home evidence; fast_charger_present/type and cable checks stay; no fixed 19.2 kW. D2 (neighbour AC EVSE) not built.
- User 2026-10-18 (E1/E2/paid): E2 — keep restores on the policy pass (≤ ~30 s after unplug; no direct trigger). Paid only when no free channel is available, or the free channel has failed repeatedly (keep the existing free-failure wait counted from the first failed free attempt; a single missed BLE ping is not enough). E1 — applicability tests the plug for start/amps; for the limit the user wants both: a car arriving home sets the policy limit before it is plugged in (saves a charge cycle), while a car that just unplugged gets its restore before driving away. Proposed stateless rule (pending user OK): for a car home and unplugged, compare two evidence timestamps — the last unplug here vs the last evidence of having been away (a fix outside the radius or a drive ending inside it). Away newer ⇒ arriving ⇒ apply the policy limit. Unplug newer ⇒ departing ⇒ restore.
- User 2026-10-18: E1 rule accepted with drive start as leaving evidence: leaving = newer of (last unplug here, last drive start inside the radius); arriving = newer of (last fix outside the radius, last drive end inside it). Home+unplugged: arriving newer ⇒ apply policy limit (before plug); leaving newer ⇒ restore. Acts within a tick of a pushed shift (TeslaMate/Fleet Telemetry); without push, Fleet covers it once outside the radius.
