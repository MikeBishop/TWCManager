## Distributor two-pass (user, 2026-10-11)
Second pass needed only when the first pass dropped an EVSE AND power is left at the end of the list.
Re-admit dropped EVSEs (smallest minimum first) while leftover >= that EVSE's minimum.
With no drops, leftover at the end means every EVSE is capped (by maxPower or controller budget), so there is nothing to do.
Deferred to the command-abstraction rework (1.4.0).
Alternative (user, preferred framing): pass 1 = admission (walk EVSEs, admit while sum of minima fits total + each controller budget; skip, don't stop, on one that doesn't fit). Pass 2 = allocate among admitted, count known.
Open choice for pass 2: equal water level clamp(L, min_i, max_i) (keeps today's equal-share fairness) vs min_i + equal share of surplus (high-minimum car gets more).
Admission order = drop policy (today: smaller maxPower dropped first). Needs hysteresis with the stop/resume state logic.
